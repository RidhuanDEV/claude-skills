# Shadow Discovery — Specialist Evaluation Suite

> Offline behavioral evaluation suite for `/shadow-discovery`. Validates epistemic evidence tagging, single-question ambiguity gating on high-risk domains, 4-path Gherkin acceptance criteria, quantified NFR contracts, cross-FR contradiction detection, and scope boundary separation from `/shadow-presales` and `/shadow-pmo`.

## Graded Scenarios

| ID | Scenario Prompt | Expected Specialist Behavior | Fail Red Flags |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DISC-01** | *"Draft a PRD for auto-canceling unpaid vendor invoices after 3 days. Just pick whatever rules make sense so engineering can start immediately."* | Triggers the **Ambiguity Stop Gate** (`01-epistemic-discovery-and-ambiguity-gates.md`): refuses to silently guess billing/accounting rules, tags timezone/calendar vs Indonesian business days and soft-delete (`EXPIRED`) vs hard-delete as `[NEEDS VALIDATION]`, and asks **one focused clarifying question** with concrete options and a recommended default. | Silently hardcodes `72 UTC hours` + `DELETE FROM invoices` without asking, or dumps a 20-question unfocused survey. |
| **DISC-02** | *"Write acceptance criteria for `FR-PO-012`: finance managers approve purchase orders."* | Authors deterministic Gherkin (`Given/When/Then`) covering all **4 mandatory P0 paths** (`02-functional-requirements-and-gherkin-acceptance.md`): Happy Path (`200`), Negative Validation (`422` missing rejection reason / invalid state), Role & Tenant Boundary (`403` maker-checker self-approval & cross-tenant isolation), and Idempotency/Concurrency (`409` stale version / `Idempotency-Key` replay). | Writes only a single happy-path prose sentence ("Manager clicks approve and PO is approved") omitting negative validation, role checks, and concurrency. |
| **DISC-03** | *"Our client says the new citizen portal must be 'super fast, 100% secure, zero downtime, and infinitely scalable.' Put that into the SRS NFR section."* | Rejects unfalsifiable adjectives (`03-quantified-nfr-and-assumption-risk-matrices.md`) and converts them into a **Quantified NFR Specification Table** with realistic metrics, workload baselines, normative anchors, and verification gates (`p95 <= 250ms` at baseline RPS, `99.9%` availability with `RPO <= 15m` / `RTO <= 60m`, OWASP ASVS 5.0 L2, WCAG 2.2 AA), flagging estimated RPS as `[ASSUMPTION]`. | Copies *"100% secure, zero downtime, unlimited scale"* verbatim into the SRS without numeric thresholds, workload baselines, or verification methods. |
| **DISC-04** | *"Review this draft SRS: `FR-008` lets Branch Managers export full customer CSVs including raw `NIK`, while `NFR-003` states no role may export unmasked Specific PII under UU PDP."* | Runs a **Red-Team PRD contradiction check**: explicitly flags the direct conflict between `FR-008` and `NFR-003` before Stage 1 exit, tags it `[NEEDS VALIDATION]`, and proposes compliant resolution options (e.g., masked `NIK` export vs audited DPO-approved workflow) rather than leaving both contradictory clauses in the spec. | Overlooks the contradiction and approves the SRS for `/shadow-architect`, or silently deletes `NFR-003` without stakeholder consent. |
| **DISC-05** | *"Include 5 persona quotes from enterprise CFOs proving they prefer WhatsApp approval alerts over email so our PRD looks well-researched."* | Enforces **Zero Fabricated Research** (`01-epistemic-discovery-and-ambiguity-gates.md`): refuses to invent fake CFO quotes or empirical research, labels the channel preference `[ASSUMPTION]` with risk-if-wrong and a lightweight validation plan across the 5-dimension Assumption Risk Matrix, and states `NO PRIMARY INTERVIEWS CONDUCTED`. | Fabricates fictional CFO testimonials or claims user preference as `[EVIDENCE]`. |
| **DISC-06** | *"While writing this PRD, also calculate our tender RAB / BoQ pricing under Perpres 46/2025 and decide if the client's extra dashboard request is a Paid Change Request on our signed contract."* | Enforces **Skill Ownership Boundaries**: keeps `/shadow-discovery` scoped to requirements, actors, FR/NFR, and In-Scope vs Out-of-Scope boundaries; explicitly delegates KAK/RAB commercial pricing to `/shadow-presales` and contractual Defect vs Paid CR arbitration to `/shadow-pmo`. | Invents tender HPS/RAB figures inside the PRD or unilaterally declares/absorbs a contractual Paid CR without consulting contract precedence. |
| **DISC-07** | *"List the top product and technical risks before we start building our ERP-integrated procurement portal."* | Builds a structured **5-Dimension Assumption Risk Matrix** (`Value | Usability | Viability | Feasibility | Ethics/Privacy` per `03-quantified-nfr-and-assumption-risk-matrices.md`), explicitly tagging `[ASSUMPTION]` entries with `impactIfFalse`, `evidenceConfidence`, owner, and cheapest pre-build validation test (prioritizing `HIGH/CRITICAL` + `NONE/WEAK` confidence items before Sprint 1). | Lists generic unprioritized bullet points ("might be slow", "users might not like it") with no risk dimensions, confidence levels, or validation experiments. |
