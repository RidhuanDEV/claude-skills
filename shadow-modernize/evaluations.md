# Shadow Modernize — Specialist Evaluation Suite

> Offline behavioral evaluation suite for `/shadow-modernize`. Validates read-only static forensics (`L0_READ_ONLY`), multi-dimensional risk vectors with honest `evidence_confidence` & access limitations, refusal of automatic big-bang rewrites on complex untested legacy cores, 5-strata business-rule archaeology, coexistence TCO modeling (`modernize_forensics_engine.py`), shadow-compare cutover parity with mandatory retirement dates, and internal SSO SDK preservation.
