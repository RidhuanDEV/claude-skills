## 2. Change Request (CR) Decision Record
- `CR ID` · `Requester` · `Contract/Baseline Precedence Check` · `Proposed Classification (Candidate)` · `Affected Modules (DB/API/FE/Mobile/Docs)` · `Critical Path Impact (Days)` · `Cost Impact (Man-Days & IDR)` · `CCB / PPK Approval Status (PENDING / APPROVED / REJECTED)` · `New Baseline Version`.

## 3. Schedule Impact & Earned Value Summary
Report deterministic CPM Critical Path alongside **Monte Carlo `P50 / P80 / P90`**, `CPI`, `SPI`, `EAC`, and active **RAID Log** blockers.

## 4. Indonesian Consulting Reports (`Laporan Pendahuluan / Antara / Akhir`) & BAST Pack
- **Laporan Pendahuluan**: Mobilization, methodology, governance charter, initial RAID & baseline schedule.
- **Laporan Antara**: Interim progress, control/code verification evidence, EVM status, CR register.
- **Laporan Akhir & BAST Gate**: Completed RTM, Staging UAT sign-off, handover inventory, remaining warranty scope.
