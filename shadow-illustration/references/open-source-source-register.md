Prefer an approved installed renderer, editor or exporter over any candidate above. If a new tool is necessary, verify ownership, license at the chosen version, release and maintenance signals, platform support, dependency risk, and required output with a small sample. Record the decision before adopting it. A tool's license does not establish rights to reference art or example files.
