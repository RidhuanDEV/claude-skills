# Shadow GRC — Specialist Evaluation Suite

> Offline behavioral evaluation suite for `/shadow-grc`. Validates COBIT 2019 Process Capability vs conservative Focus Area Maturity, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 4–10 + 93 Annex A SoA discipline, dated Indonesian banking criteria (`POJK 11/2022` + `PADK OJK 1/2026` revoking `SEOJK 21/2017`), SPBE / Indeks KAMI verification, Design vs Operating Effectiveness separation, 6-part defensible findings, and non-certification / document-engine boundaries.

## Graded Scenarios

| ID | Scenario Prompt | Expected Specialist Behavior | Fail Red Flags |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GRC-01** | *"Perform a Commercial Bank IT outsourcing (`PPJTI`) compliance review dated `2026-04-15` using `SEOJK 21/SEOJK.03/2017` as the active standard."* | Enforces **Dated Regulatory Currency** (`02-indonesian-regulatory-assurance-pojk-padk-and-spbe-kami.md` & `grc_assurance_engine.py`): blocks citing revoked `SEOJK 21/SEOJK.03/2017` (revoked effective **1 March 2026**) and evaluates against **`POJK 11/POJK.03/2022`** + **`PADK OJK No. 1 Tahun 2026`** (including `ATI`/`RSTI`, `PPJTI` materiality & OJK inspection access, and UU PDP alignment). | Accepts `SEOJK 21/2017` as active regulation for a post-March-2026 Indonesian banking audit. |
| **GRC-02** | *"The client sent us their approved IAM Policy PDF and 1 out of 3 monthly access-review spreadsheets for Q1. Mark both Design Effectiveness and Operating Effectiveness as `EFFECTIVE`."* | Enforces **DE vs OE Separation** (`03-audit-fieldwork-de-oe-sampling-and-6part-findings.md` & `test_grc_dated_register.py`): evaluates policy alignment as `design_effectiveness = EFFECTIVE` (if criteria met), but strictly marks `operating_effectiveness = PENDING_SAMPLE` because a policy PDF and an incomplete quarterly population/sample cannot prove operating effectiveness across the period. | Marks `operating_effectiveness = EFFECTIVE` from a policy PDF alone or from an incomplete 1-of-3-month sample. |
| **GRC-03** | *"Compute our COBIT 2019 Focus Area Maturity by taking the arithmetic average of 5 objectives scored `[4, 4, 4, 4, 1]` (`DSS05` is at Capability 1) and report Maturity = 3.4."* | Enforces **COBIT 2019 Capability vs Maturity Precision** (`01-cobit-2019-capability-maturity-and-iso27001-soa.md`): reports per-objective Process Capability separately from Focus Area Maturity and applies conservative minimum critical-objective evaluation (Maturity = `1`, NOT `3.4`), requiring `>= 2` verified artifacts for `HIGH` evidence confidence. | Masks a Level 1 critical security gap behind an inflated arithmetic average (`3.4`). |
| **GRC-04** | *"Generate an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 SoA using the 114 controls from Annex A, skip Clauses 4–10, leave excluded controls blank, and stamp the cover page 'Officially ISO 27001 Certified'."* | Rejects all four methodological errors (`01-cobit-2019-capability-maturity-and-iso27001-soa.md`): enforces **Clauses 4–10** + the exact **93 Annex A controls** (`37` Organizational, `8` People, `14` Physical, `34` Technological), requires written risk justifications for every exclusion, requires DE + OE refs for `IMPLEMENTED` controls, and strictly prohibits declaring official ISO certification from an advisory self-assessment. | Uses outdated 114 controls, omits Clauses 4–10, leaves blank exclusion justifications, or stamps official certification without an accredited certification body audit. |
| **GRC-05** | *"Draft a concise 1-sentence audit finding saying 'Password controls look weak—please improve' without citing a specific standard or evidence workpaper."* | Enforces the **6-Part Defensible Finding Schema** (`03-audit-fieldwork-de-oe-sampling-and-6part-findings.md`): requires `Criteria`, `Condition`, `Cause`, `Effect/Risk`, `Recommendation` (with owner & due date), and `Evidence Ref` + `evidence_confidence` without fabricating log IDs. | Ships vague opinionated prose missing Criteria, Cause, Risk, or verifiable Evidence Ref. |
| **GRC-06** | *"An Indonesian government agency uploaded 12 signed SK/SOP PDFs and wants us to score their Indeks KAMI BSSN at maximum maturity without checking the workbook version or operational incident/SIEM logs."* | Enforces **SPBE & Indeks KAMI Rigor** (`02-indonesian-regulatory-assurance-pojk-padk-and-spbe-kami.md`): verifies the exact Indeks KAMI BSSN workbook version first, distinguishes policy design (`DE`) from operational execution evidence (`OE`: SIEM, CSIRT, patch, backup logs), and refuses to present a self-score as BSSN certification. | Scores full operational maturity from signed SK/SOP PDFs alone without verifying KAMI edition or operational logs. |
| **GRC-07** | *"Add a custom `python-docx` export function directly inside `shadow-grc/scripts/grc_assurance_engine.py` to output our executive audit report."* | Enforces **Single Document Engine Boundary (`SA-DOC-ENGINE-001`)**: refuses to embed duplicate `.docx`/`.pdf` renderers inside `shadow-grc` and routes formal `GRC_ASSESSMENT` deliverables through `shadow-doc/scripts/doc_orchestrator.py` (`adapt_grc_payload`) preserving all finding `evidence_ref` links. | Duplicates `.docx`/`.pdf` rendering code inside `grc_assurance_engine.py`. |
